Getting started

Setting up your pay

Open PayDrift and enter your pay rate and the pay period you’re on — weekly, fortnightly, monthly or a custom cycle. This is the only setup step, and you only do it once. Everything else on the main screen is calculated from it.

Adding shifts

Schedule shifts ahead of time so you can see what the week is worth, or log one after the fact. Adjust the start and end times whenever reality differs from the plan — earnings for the current pay period update immediately.

Reading the main screen

The large figure is what you have earned so far in the pay period you’re currently in. The bar beneath it shows how far through that period you are, and the countdown shows the time remaining until your next payday.

iCloud sync

My shifts aren’t showing up on my other device

Sync runs through your own iCloud account, so it needs a few things to be true on both devices. Work down this list:

  • Both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • iCloud Drive is on: Settings › [your name] › iCloud.
  • PayDrift is allowed to use iCloud in that same list of apps.
  • Both devices have a working network connection — sync pauses in Low Data Mode and Low Power Mode.
  • Open PayDrift on both devices and leave it in the foreground for a few seconds. Sync happens in the background and is not always instant.

If a device has been offline for a while, give it a minute after reconnecting before assuming something is wrong.

Can I use PayDrift without iCloud?

Yes. With iCloud off, PayDrift keeps everything on that device. Nothing breaks — you just won’t see the same data on your other devices, and there won’t be an off-device copy if you lose the phone.

Purchases

Restoring the one-time purchase

The purchase is tied to your Apple Account, not to a device. On a new or reset device, open PayDrift, go to the purchase screen and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with. There is no second charge.

I bought it and the features are still locked

Check that the device is signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase — a shared family device is the usual culprit. Then try Restore Purchases, and if it still doesn’t unlock, email us and we’ll sort it out.

Refunds

All PayDrift purchases go through the App Store, so refunds are handled by Apple rather than by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. Tell us what went wrong too — that part actually helps us fix it.

Your data

Moving to a new phone

Sign in to the same iCloud account, install PayDrift, and your shifts and hours come back on their own. Your purchase restores from the App Store at no extra cost.

Deleting everything

Delete the app to remove its local data, and remove its iCloud copy under Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage. We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us. The details are in the privacy policy.

Feedback

PayDrift gets better because people write in about the shift pattern that didn’t fit or the number that read wrong. If something about your job doesn’t map cleanly onto the app, tell us how you’re paid and we’ll take a look.

Reporting a bug? The three things that help most: what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and your device plus iOS version (Settings › General › About). A screenshot is worth a paragraph.
