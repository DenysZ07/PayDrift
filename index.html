FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is PayDrift free?

Yes. Scheduling and tracking shifts, logging hours, seeing your earnings for the current pay period and counting down to payday are all free, with no ads.

What does the one-time purchase add?

Average earnings per week, a side-by-side look at this pay period and the last one, and a projected annual income based on what you actually work.

Can I track more than one job?

Yes. Add as many jobs as you work. Each one keeps its own pay rate, pay period and payday, and gets its own card on the Overview screen — so nothing gets mixed together.

Is there a subscription?

No. PayDrift is a single one-time purchase. You pay once and it’s yours, including on the other Apple devices signed in to the same Apple ID.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and in your own private iCloud so it stays in step across your Apple devices. PayDrift has no server of its own and never collects your data.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. If you’re signed in to iCloud, sync just works; if you’re not, PayDrift keeps everything locally on your device.

What happens when I get a new phone?

Sign in to the same iCloud account, install PayDrift, and your shifts and hours come back with it. Your purchase restores from the App Store at no extra cost.
