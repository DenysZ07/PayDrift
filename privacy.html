Privacy Policy

How PayDrift handles your information — which is, as much as possible, by not handling it at all.

Last updated: August 8, 2026

The short version. PayDrift has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your shifts, hours and pay details are stored on your device and, if you have iCloud switched on, in your own private iCloud account. We do not collect them, we cannot read them, and we never sell or share them. There are no ads and no third-party trackers in the app.

Who this policy is from

This policy covers the PayDrift app for Apple devices and this website. Throughout, “we” and “us” mean the developer of PayDrift, and “you” means the person using it.

What PayDrift stores

To do its job, PayDrift keeps the information you enter into it:

  • Your pay rate and pay period settings
  • Shifts you schedule, and the hours you log against them
  • The earnings PayDrift calculates from the two above
  • Your in-app preferences, such as which currency and theme you use

This information is created by you, for you. It is not transmitted to us.

Where your data lives

On your device

By default, everything PayDrift stores lives in the app’s own storage on your device, protected by the operating system in the same way as any other app’s data.

In your private iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud and have iCloud enabled for PayDrift, your data is also synced to the private database of your personal iCloud account so it stays consistent across the Apple devices signed in to the same Apple Account. That sync runs between your devices and Apple — it does not pass through any server of ours, and we have no access to the contents of your private iCloud database. Apple’s handling of that data is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

You can turn iCloud sync off at any time in iOS Settings, in which case PayDrift keeps working entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. PayDrift contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, no attribution or fingerprinting tools, and no crash-reporting service that sends your data to us. We do not build a profile of you, we do not know what you earn, and there is no account for us to attach anything to.

Because PayDrift is distributed through the App Store, Apple provides us with the same aggregated, anonymised reporting it gives every developer — things like how many people downloaded the app in a given country, or how many devices it runs on. These reports come from Apple, contain no personal information, and cannot be traced back to an individual. You can limit what Apple shares in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Purchases

PayDrift offers a single optional one-time purchase. That transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, card number or billing address. Apple tells the app whether the purchase is active so that it can unlock the paid features; we receive only Apple’s aggregated sales reporting.

Permissions

PayDrift asks only for permissions it needs to do the thing you asked it to do — for example notification permission, if you want a reminder about an upcoming shift or an alert on payday. Every such prompt is optional, you can decline it, and you can change your mind later in iOS Settings. Declining a permission never disables the core of the app.

Who we share data with

No one. There are no data brokers, no advertising partners, no analytics vendors, and no “trusted third parties” in this policy, because there is no data on our side to share. The only company involved at all is Apple, in its role as the platform, the App Store and your iCloud provider.

Deleting your data

Because your data is yours, deleting it is entirely in your hands:

  • Delete individual shifts or reset your settings inside the app.
  • Delete the app from your device to remove its local data.
  • Remove the synced copy in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage, where PayDrift’s iCloud data can be deleted like any other app’s.

There is nothing to email us about and no deletion request to file — we hold no copy to delete.

This website

This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and embeds no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. Your browser’s theme choice is stored locally on your own device and never leaves it. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

PayDrift is a general-purpose tool for tracking your own working hours and is not directed at children. Since we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect any from children either.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete the personal data a company holds about you, and to know whether it is sold. In PayDrift’s case those rights are satisfied by design: we hold no personal data about you, we sell nothing, and the data itself stays under your direct control on your own devices and in your own iCloud account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date. If a future version of the app were ever to change what it stores or sends, that change would be described here before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about privacy in PayDrift generally, are welcome at support@paydrift.app.
